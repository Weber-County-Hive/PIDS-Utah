What's a PID, in plain words?

A Public Infrastructure District (PID) is a small taxing district a city or county creates around a new development, usually at the developer's request. The district borrows money by selling bonds to pay for things like roads, water and sewer lines.

That debt is paid back over many years through an extra tax charged to the properties inside the district's boundary, which usually means the people who later buy homes or businesses there. Those owners also pay all the regular property taxes everyone else pays.

Wildcat Square, in Plain Words

A short, simple version of the full case file.

Posted Oct. 31, 2026 · Updated Oct. 31, 2026
Want all the details and sources? Read the full case file
Share on Facebook

What is happening?

Weber State University wants more student apartments on its Ogden campus. A company called Keller Development Group would build them on a practice field. Weber State would lease the land to the company for 99 years.

The city of Ogden has a group called the Redevelopment Agency, or RDA. The City Council members are its board. The RDA wants to help pay for the project with future taxes.

How does the money work?

Right now, the land pays no tax because it belongs to the state.

Once a private company builds apartments there, the building will pay a tax called a privilege tax. It works like a property tax.

For 15 years, most of that tax would go to the RDA instead of the usual places. The RDA could collect up to $3,330,116. Most of it would pay the builder back for the costly job of digging out old dirt and debris on the site. The builder only gets paid after the building is done.

After 15 years, all of the tax goes back to the normal places, like schools and the city.

Whose money is it?

Over 15 years, these three give up part of the new tax. Each one has to agree in its own public vote.

Ogden School District$2.19 million
Ogden City$775,000
Weber County$361,000

The school district gives about 2 of every 3 dollars.

This is a good question to ask at the hearing: what changed?

What do people say?

For it: the land pays nothing now, so this adds a new tax for the future. Weber State says its student housing is full, with long waitlists.

Concerns: some residents said it takes away campus green space, costs the public money, and pulls growth away from downtown Ogden.

What doesn't match?

The city's own papers call it 103 apartments in some places and 513 beds in another. They also give two different dates for the first vote. Those are good questions to ask.

How can I take part?

Go to the public hearing on Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, at 6 p.m., in the Ogden City Council Chambers, 3rd floor, 2549 Washington Blvd. You can speak, or turn in a written comment before the hearing.
